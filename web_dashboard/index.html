📋 Methodology & Limitations
Transparency about data sources, methods, and known issues
⚠ Synthetic Dataset: This dataset was generated synthetically with near-uniform distributions. All categorical dimensions show approximately equal proportions. Findings should NOT be used for real-world career or business decisions.
# Limitation Impact Status
1 Synthetic uniform data Near-equal distributions — no real imbalances Critical
2 skills_demand ↔ ai_jobs join failure (4.1%) Job-level skill enrichment unavailable Critical
3 2025–2026 projected years (28.4% of rows) Must not be treated as observed history Medium
4 employment_type = Full-time only Zero variance — excluded from analysis Medium
5 Salary varies only by experience_level Country/industry/role salary differences <$1,100 Medium
6 6 countries, 6 roles, 11 skills only Narrow scope — not globally representative Medium
7 Two independent data populations 50k sample ≠ country_ai_trends market data Documented
